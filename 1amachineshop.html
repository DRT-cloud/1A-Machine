What We Do

Capabilities

From a single replacement bushing to a short run of CNC-milled brackets — repair work, prototypes, and custom fabrication. If a job is better suited to a larger shop, we'll say so.

01 — CNC
CNC Milling
3-axis vertical machining on the Fadal 3016L. Programmed from CAD/CAM for consistent, repeatable results on complex geometry, pockets, contours, and precision flat work.
Fadal 3016L VMC  ·  16″ × 30″ travel  ·  ±0.001″ on CNC features
02 — Turning
Manual Lathe Work
Shafts, bushings, pins, sleeves, flanges, and custom fittings. Facing, threading, boring, taper turning, and knurling. New parts and repair of worn or damaged components.
Engine lathe  ·  15″ swing × 40″ between centers
03 — Milling
Manual Milling
Keyways, slots, flats, drilling patterns, and secondary operations that don't justify CNC setup time. Fast and practical for one-off repair work and small modifications to existing parts.
Knee mill  ·  9″ × 40″ table
04 — Repair
Repair Machining
Bring in a worn, broken, or obsolete part. We measure it, model if needed, and make a replacement or repair it in-house. Common applications: valve components, implement parts, hydraulic cylinder components, and pump shafts.
Reverse engineering  ·  Sample parts accepted
05 — Prototype
Prototype & Short-Run
From napkin sketch or CAD file to finished part. CAD modeling, CAM programming, 3D-printed fit-check models, then machined final parts — all in house. Accepts STEP, IGES, DXF, DWG, STL, and PDF drawings.
SolidWorks / Fusion 360  ·  3D printing for fit-check
06 — Fab
Welding & Fabrication
MIG, TIG, and stick welding on steel, aluminum, and stainless. Brackets, frames, mounts, guards, and small assemblies. 30-ton hydraulic press for pressing, straightening, and assembly operations.
MIG  ·  TIG  ·  Stick  ·  30-ton press

The Shop

About 1A Machine Shop

1A Machine Shop is owned and operated by Weston Allenbaugh out of Pawnee, Oklahoma. When you call, you talk to the person making your parts. When you have a question about material, tolerance, or design, you get an answer from the machinist — not a sales rep reading from a script.

The shop runs a Fadal 3016L CNC vertical machining center alongside manual lathe and milling machines, welding equipment, a 30-ton hydraulic press, and 3D printing capability. That combination handles everything from CNC-programmed precision work to hands-on repair machining and fabrication — the flexibility that large production shops can't match and small welding shops can't offer.

Located in Pawnee, we're 20 minutes from Cushing, 25 minutes from Stillwater, and within easy reach of Perry, Drumright, Ponca City, and the wider central Oklahoma area. Whether it's an emergency valve repair for a Cushing pipeline operator or a prototype bracket for an engineering program, the work gets personal attention and fast turnaround.

We're set up for custom work, repair machining, short runs, and prototypes — not high-volume production. If your job is a better fit for a larger shop, we'll tell you that up front.
